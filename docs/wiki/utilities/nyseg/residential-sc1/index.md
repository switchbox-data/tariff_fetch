## Overview

**SC1** is **New York State Electric & Gas (NYSEG)'s default residential electric rate** for non–demand-metered customers. NYSEG serves Upstate New York (East, West, and Lower Hudson Valley). Under SC1, customers can take NYSEG Supply Service (NSS) as default—a managed mix of supply at a variable price—or choose an ESCO for supply.

This is a **flat, non–time-of-use rate** for delivery: one customer charge, one bill issuance charge, one energy charge, plus revenue decoupling, make-whole energy, transition charge, system benefits charge, clean energy standard, and various riders. **Supply Service Charge** varies by **territory** (East, Lower Hudson Valley, West). Riders include rate adjustment mechanism, earnings adjustment mechanism, DLM, CBC (solar), EV Make Ready, late payment/waived fees surcharge, arrears relief, and recovery charge. **Residential Agricultural Discount (RAD)** applies for RAD participants (lookup).
