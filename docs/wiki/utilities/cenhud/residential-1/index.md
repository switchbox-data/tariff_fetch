## Overview

**Rate 1** is **Central Hudson Gas & Electric** default residential electric rate. Central Hudson serves the Mid-Hudson Valley (Ulster, Dutchess, and surrounding counties). This is a **flat, non-TOU** rate: customer charge, energy charge (delivery), allocation of MFC lost revenue, base MFC supply charge, MFC administration charge, miscellaneous charges, market price charge, revenue decoupling mechanism, transition adjustment, purchased power adjustment, electric bill credit, market price adjustment, system benefits charge, earnings adjustment mechanism surcharge, rate adjustment mechanism surcharge, minimum charge, low-income bill discount, and customer benefit contribution (solar). **Low-income** tiers (electric heat vs non-electric heat, Tier 1–4) are in the tariff (see lowIncomeBillDiscount2033 property).

Fixed monthly charge.

### Energy Charge

Volumetric delivery rate; recovers distribution revenue requirement.

### Merchant Function (MFC) Components

Allocation of MFC Lost Revenue, Base MFC Supply Charge, MFC Administration Charge—supply-side administration and true-ups.

### Miscellaneous Charges / Market Price Charge / Market Price Adjustment / Purchased Power Adjustment / Electric Bill Credit

Supply and delivery adjustments; market price and purchased power are typically variable (lookups).

### Revenue Decoupling Mechanism / Transition Adjustment

Decoupling and restructuring adjustments.

### System Benefits Charge

NYSERDA-related; lookup or fixed.

### Earnings Adjustment Mechanism / Rate Adjustment Mechanism Surcharges

Delivery/revenue adjustment mechanisms.

### Minimum Charge

Floor on bill.

### Low Income Bill Discount

Tiered discount for qualifying customers (see property).

### Customer Benefit Contribution

CBC for solar customers (VDER).

## Example Bill Calculation

500 kWh, no solar, no LMI: Customer $22.50 + Energy (500 × rate) + MFC components + Market Price (lookup) + RDM, Transition, SBC, EAM, RAM, etc. Minimum applies.

## Summary: What You Need to Know

1. **Rate 1 is flat** — No TOU, no seasonal delivery tiers in the base energy charge.
2. **MFC** is broken into multiple components (lost revenue, base supply, administration).
3. **Market Price / Purchased Power / Electric Bill Credit** drive variable supply-side amounts.
4. **RDM, Transition, SBC, EAM, RAM** are standard NY adjustment and policy charges.
5. **Low-income** and **CBC** (solar) are eligibility-based.
